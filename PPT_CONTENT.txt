SLIDE 5 – YOLO ALGORITHM
────────────────────────────────────────
HEADING: How YOLOv8 Works

BULLETS:
• Single-pass detector – processes the entire image in one forward pass
• Image divided into S×S grid (e.g., 20×20 for 640px input)
• Each cell predicts bounding boxes (cx, cy, w, h) + class probabilities
• Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) removes duplicate detections
• YOLOv8 adds: Anchor-free head, C2f backbone, decoupled cls/reg heads
• Pretrained on COCO (118K images, 80 classes) – No extra training needed!

✓ No custom training needed (COCO-pretrained)

✓ Real-time on consumer hardware
